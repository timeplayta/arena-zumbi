# What's new — Arena Zumbi

## Weapon sights fixed (11/14/2026)
- Vespa SMG-9, AX-4 Rifle and Titan M-60: the rear sight is now an open ring. When aiming, you see the target and the front sight in the center, without the gun blocking the screen.
- The on-screen crosshair stays visible when aiming with these weapons.

## New version notice and always up-to-date online (11/13/2026)
- When an update comes out, anyone with the previous version open sees the "New version available" notice. On the website, the button updates right away. In the app, just close and reopen the game.
- Online (co-op and Infection) only works on the latest version of the game, so everyone in a room plays the same version.

## Online on the current version only (11/13/2026)
- The online servers (co-op and Infection) now only work on the current version of the game: website, new Android app and new Windows app.
- Previous versions of the app no longer connect online. Download the new version at machine-gamesdev.itch.io/arena-zumbi.

## Service change (11/13/2026)
- Arena Zumbi has changed services. A new version of the app is out for Android and Windows.
- When you open a previous version, a notice appears with the "Download new version" button, which opens the game's itch.io page.
- Your progress stays saved in your account.

## Official trailer (11/11/2026)
- The official Arena Zumbi trailer is out on YouTube!
- On the Mission tab, tap the trailer card to watch it. The video opens in your language.

## Infection: infected on stairs (11/10/2026)
- A player-controlled infected no longer gets stuck in the sky or walks in place when climbing the village stairs and terraces.
- Humans now see the infected in the right place on stairs and terraces.
- AI zombies no longer get stuck on walls and railings: they look for another route.

## 4:3 screens and custom resolutions (11/10/2026)
- On 4:3, 5:4 and 16:10 (e.g. 1440x1080) the gun is back to its normal size instead of huge.
- When aiming, the gun sits straight in the center of the screen; shots and tracers come from the right muzzle, no longer skewed to the left.

## No more stutters: loading screen (11/10/2026)
- New loading screen: guns, knife, potions and the Italian village are built and the graphics are prepared before the menu.
- Pulling out the knife, the heal or the fire potion, turning on the flashlight and switching guns no longer freeze the screen.
- Switching guns is lighter: the gun is no longer rebuilt on every switch.
- Fixed a rare knife error in Infection before the match starts.

## Infection: end screen shows the winner (11/09/2026)
- At the end of Infection, the screen shows who won: "Humans win" (in green) or "Zombies win".
- The match time no longer shows as negative when a match ends right at the start.

## Co-op: downed notice (11/09/2026)
- When you go down in co-op, "You respawn next wave" shows in the middle of the screen, for both the host and guests.
- The Infection countdown sits a little lower so it doesn't cover the game notices.

## Infection: respawn countdown (11/09/2026)
- Zombie taken down: the "Back in 5… 4…" countdown shows big in the middle of the screen.
- English and Spanish: the Infection bar with humans, zombies and time is now translated.

## Fixes (11/09/2026)
- Phone in landscape: the game over and pause screens fit without scrolling; the key hint is hidden on touch.
- 4:3, 16:10 and portrait screens: the gun and arms show in full, resting at the bottom.
- Full review: co-op (damage after respawning), Infection, waves, guns and bosses retested with no errors.

## Infection: heavy hit (11/08/2026)
- The zombie heavy hit (right button) counts as 1.5 hits.
- Without a vest: 4 normal or 3 heavy hits. With a vest: 6 normal or 4 heavy hits.

## Infection: zombie hits (11/08/2026)
- A zombie infects a human without a vest in 4 hits, and with a vest in 6 hits.
- Normal and heavy hits count the same.

## See-through optical sight (11/08/2026)
- The red dot sight is now a tube with glass: you can see the target through it, with the red dot in the center.

## Co-op fix (11/08/2026)
- Players who died and respawned in co-op stayed invincible forever. Now the protection lasts only 3 seconds.

## Test update (11/02/2026)
- Testing automatic updates on the portable .exe. If you are reading this, it worked!

## .exe fix (11/02/2026)
- The .exe was opening an old version of the game; now it opens the latest one, with languages.

## Languages: English, Spanish and Portuguese (11/02/2026)
- Pick the language in Settings > Interface > Language, or on the sign-in screen.
- The whole game switches instantly: menus, store, HUD, alerts and messages.
- The first time, the game uses your device language (English unless it is Spanish or Portuguese).
