# What's new — Arena Zumbi

## Infection: end screen shows the winner (11/09/2026)
- At the end of Infection, the screen shows who won: "Humans win" (in green) or "Zombies win".
- The match time no longer shows as negative when a match ends right at the start.

## Co-op: downed notice (11/09/2026)
- When you go down in co-op, "You respawn next wave" shows in the middle of the screen, for both the host and guests.
- The Infection countdown sits a little lower so it doesn't cover the game notices.

## Infection: respawn countdown (11/09/2026)
- Zombie taken down: the "Back in 5… 4…" countdown shows big in the middle of the screen.
- English and Spanish: the Infection bar with humans, zombies and time is now translated.

## Fixes (11/09/2026)
- Phone in landscape: the game over and pause screens fit without scrolling; the key hint is hidden on touch.
- 4:3, 16:10 and portrait screens: the gun and arms show in full, resting at the bottom.
- Full review: co-op (damage after respawning), Infection, waves, guns and bosses retested with no errors.

## Infection: heavy hit (11/08/2026)
- The zombie heavy hit (right button) counts as 1.5 hits.
- Without a vest: 4 normal or 3 heavy hits. With a vest: 6 normal or 4 heavy hits.

## Infection: zombie hits (11/08/2026)
- A zombie infects a human without a vest in 4 hits, and with a vest in 6 hits.
- Normal and heavy hits count the same.

## See-through optical sight (11/08/2026)
- The red dot sight is now a tube with glass: you can see the target through it, with the red dot in the center.

## Co-op fix (11/08/2026)
- Players who died and respawned in co-op stayed invincible forever. Now the protection lasts only 3 seconds.

## Test update (11/02/2026)
- Testing automatic updates on the portable .exe. If you are reading this, it worked!

## .exe fix (11/02/2026)
- The .exe was opening an old version of the game; now it opens the latest one, with languages.

## Languages: English, Spanish and Portuguese (11/02/2026)
- Pick the language in Settings > Interface > Language, or on the sign-in screen.
- The whole game switches instantly: menus, store, HUD, alerts and messages.
- The first time, the game uses your device language (English unless it is Spanish or Portuguese).
